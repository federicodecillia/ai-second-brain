# 00_Inbox — drop zone

Dump anything here that you don't want to file by hand: a link, a half-formed idea, a pasted message, a transcribed voice memo, a screenshot turned into text. Don't organize it — that's the agent's job.

- **Quick ideas (1-2 lines)** → jot them in `appunti.md` (a persistent buffer, one line per thought). Works from your computer or from Obsidian on your phone.
- **Anything bigger** (an article, a draft, a document) → drop it as its own note here.

The autopilot routine ([maintenance.md](../maintenance.md)) reads this folder, files each item into the right PARA bucket, links it, notes it in today's `Daily/` entry, and empties the inbox. You can also trigger it anytime: *"run maintenance.md"* or *"file my inbox"*.

Keep this `_README.md`; everything else here is temporary.
